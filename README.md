### Thematic Focus: Music, Sound, and Audio Culture
Throughout this semester, Group A2 investigates how musical expression, recorded sound, acoustic artifacts, and sonic cultures are captured, quantified, digitized, curated, and governed as data. Music sits at the complex intersection of artistic subjectivity, continuous physical acoustics (analog sound waves), discrete symbolic notation (scores and MIDI), industrial copyright enclosures (recording labels and streaming algorithms), and community cultural heritage (folk traditions, oral histories, and sacred sounds).
